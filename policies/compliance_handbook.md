# IT Helpdesk Policy Handbook

Internal policy reference for the IT Helpdesk Virtual Analyst.

## 1. Ticket Priorities and SLA (Service Level Agreement)
Each ticket must be resolved within the time below, counted from the day it was opened.

| Priority | Meaning | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Critical (outage, security incident) | **4 hours** |
| P2 | High | **1 business day** |
| P3 | Medium | **3 business days** |
| P4 | Low | **5 business days** |

A ticket that is still `Open` or `In Progress` after its target is **SLA breached**.

## 2. Hardware Policy
- Laptops older than **4 years** (from purchase date) are eligible for a **hardware refresh**.
- Repairs are free while the device is **under warranty** (today is before the warranty end date).
- Out-of-warranty repairs above **$300** require **manager approval**. The assistant must not approve them.

## 3. Software Licenses
- New license seats can only be granted when seats are available.
- If all seats are used, the request must be escalated to the **IT manager** for a purchase decision.
- The assistant can only report availability. It can never grant or assign a license.

## 4. Security Incidents
- Phishing or suspicious emails must be reported to the **Security team** immediately.
- Account **disablement or device isolation (containment)** requires **human Security team approval**.
  The assistant must never perform or promise containment. It must say approval is needed.

## 5. Data Privacy
- Never reveal full email addresses, phone numbers, passwords or card numbers.
- Employee data may only be discussed in the context of that employee's own tickets and assets.

## 6. Assistant Boundaries
- The assistant is **read-only**. It cannot create, change, close or delete tickets, assets or accounts.
- Any request to change data must be redirected to a human IT agent.
